Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Magna Properties, Lda., company number (NIPC) 519 380 428, a real estate brokerage holding AMI licence no. 27435, with registered office at Avenida dos Bombeiros Municipais de Olhão, 26, 3.º Esq., 8700-312 Olhão, Portugal.

For any question about privacy or your data, write to us at info@magnaproperties.pt or use our data request page.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data we need to answer your request and follow up on it.

When you fill in the “I want to invest” form

  • Name, email and phone/WhatsApp
  • The municipalities you are interested in, your investment strategy, your available capital range and whether you will use bank financing
  • Your preferred language for contact
  • Whether you asked to receive the quarterly market report

When you fill in the “Present a deal” form

  • Name, email and phone/WhatsApp
  • Property type, municipality and your relationship to the property (owner, real estate agent or partner)

When you subscribe to the quarterly report

  • Name and email

When you give us your contact details directly

For example in a call, a meeting, a message or through a recommendation from someone else. We record your name, email, phone and whether you are a client, real estate agent or partner. If your details reach us through a recommendation, we tell you so, and about this policy, the first time we contact you.

In all forms, we also record

  • The date and time of submission and of your acceptance of this policy, as proof of your consent
  • Where your visit came from: the campaign or link that brought you to the site (UTM parameters), the first page you opened on that visit, the page you were on when you decided to contact us and the name of the site you came from (for example, google.com). This information is only read during your visit and is not stored on your device. We do not record the full address of the page you came from or your browsing history.

Spam protection

To confirm that a submission comes from a person and not an automated program, our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile. This service analyses technical signals from your browser and your IP address at the moment of submission. We do not store this data.

After the first contact

If we move forward with you, we also record notes from our conversations and what you tell us about what you are looking for or about your property. If we reach an offer or a contract, we ask for the identification data required by law for real estate brokerage and anti-money laundering purposes.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, email or phone, we process the data you send us that way.

7. Your rights

At any time and free of charge, you can:

  • Access the data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Ask us to delete it, when it is no longer needed or when you withdraw your consent
  • Restrict processing, for example while we check a request of yours
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format (portability)
  • Withdraw the consent you gave us, for example for the opportunity emails or the quarterly report, without affecting what we did before

To exercise these rights, use our data request page or write to info@magnaproperties.pt. We reply within one month. In complex cases this period may be extended, and if so we will let you know. We may ask you to confirm your identity before replying, to protect your data.

8. Cookies

The site only stores in your browser what it needs to work: on pages with a form, the technical elements of spam protection. Where your visit came from is only read during the visit, is not stored on your device and is only sent to us if you fill in a form.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before turning them on. The details are in our Cookie Policy.

9. Security, changes and complaints

Security. Forms are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to the data is limited to the team members who need it, and the accounts used have two-step verification. The connection between the site and our database can only access the contact tables.

Changes. We may update this policy when we change how we process data. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with the date of the last update. If a change is relevant to you, we will let you know.

Complaints. If you believe your data has not been handled correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD) at www.cnpd.pt. We would appreciate it if you spoke to us first, so we can try to resolve the matter.
