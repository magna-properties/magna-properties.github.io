Frequently asked questions

Before you invest.

How much capital do I need to invest with you?

We work with deals from €500,000. Tell us your range in the form to receive deals that match your profile.

How are you paid?

The pre-assessment and the Opportunity Sheet are free of charge for you. The Premium Investment Dossier is a paid service. When you buy, the commission is usually paid by the seller; if a deal is different, we tell you in writing before any commitment.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Opportunity sheets show estimates in three scenarios: conservative, base and optimistic. To reduce uncertainty before you go ahead, we recommend the premium investment dossier: it includes an analysis by our architect, with meetings at the Town Hall whenever needed, to confirm what the property allows. It is not financial advice or a guarantee of results.

Can I invest from abroad?

Yes. We do video viewings, help with your Portuguese tax number (NIF), bank account and power of attorney with legal partners, and manage the works with our partners.
