Frequently asked questions

Before you sell.

Is there a charge for the valuation?

No. The visit and the valuation are free and come with no commitment.

What properties do you take on?

Apartments, houses, buildings and urban land for development in Loulé, Faro and Olhão.

The property needs work. Can I still sell it?

Yes. Many of our investors are looking for exactly that: properties to renovate.

I have a plot but don't know what can be built on it. Can you help?

Yes. We do a first reading of what the municipal master plan (PDM) allows on your plot and, at the meeting, explain how to take that analysis further with our partner architect.

How long does it take to sell?

It depends on the type of property, the area and the price. At the meeting we give you a realistic timeline, based on recent sales in your area.

What documents do I need to sell?

The property tax record (caderneta predial), the land registry certificate (certidão permanente), the energy certificate and the occupancy permit (licença de utilização). If anything is missing, we help you obtain it.

Can the sale be kept confidential?

Yes. If you prefer, we present the property only to our investor network, with no public listing.
