Frequently asked questions

Before you invest.

How much capital do I need?

We work with a minimum investment of €500,000.

What types of property do you work with?

Apartments, houses and buildings to renovate and resell, and urban land for development. In Loulé, Faro and Olhão.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. The figures we share are estimates. To reduce uncertainty, our partner architect can review the property before you commit, as a separate service. This is not financial advice.

Can I invest from abroad?

Yes. We run video viewings, help you set up your Portuguese tax number (NIF), bank account and power of attorney with our legal partners, and oversee the works with our construction partners.

Can I buy through a company?

Yes. Buying through a company can change the taxes on the transaction, for example the property transfer tax (IMT) when buying to resell. We recommend confirming the tax treatment with your accountant.

How long does a renovation project take?

It depends on the property, the permitting and the works. Before you commit, we give you an estimated timeline for each phase.

Do you help find a contractor and oversee the works?

Yes. We work with the same construction partners on every deal and oversee the works with you through to the sale.
